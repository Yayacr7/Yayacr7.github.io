Five habits under pressure

  1. Pause two seconds before you answer.
  2. Answer first in one sentence, then give one or two reasons.
  3. Clarify if the question is unclear.
  4. "I don't know, I'll send it tomorrow" beats a confident guess.
  5. Steer back to what you're sure of, honestly.

Say your answers out loud. Practising silently in your head doesn't build the same calm.
