Before you paste

About to paste something into an AI tool? Check it first. This finds emails, phone numbers, card numbers, ID numbers and passwords or keys, and gives you a cleaned copy. It runs only on your device: nothing is sent anywhere.

Prompt library

Keep your best prompts in one place instead of lost in old chats. Saved on this device only. Re-test each one every month: AI tools change, and a prompt that worked last month can quietly stop working.

Estimates and checks, not guarantees: the paste check can miss things, so read before you share. Learn the habits in the lesson Using AI at work without the headaches.
