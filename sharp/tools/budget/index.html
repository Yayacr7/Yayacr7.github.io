    What these words mean
    Income
    Money that comes in after tax: your take-home pay plus any other money, like a side job.
    Expenses
    Everything you spent. Savings are not counted here, because saving is money you keep.
    Budget
    The most you plan to spend on a category in a month.
    Needs
    Things you must pay for: home, bills, food, getting to work, debt payments.
    Wants
    Things you choose: eating out, shopping, subscriptions, fun.
    Remaining
    What is left after spending and saving. If it is below $0, you spent more than you earned.
    Savings rate
    The share of your income you save. $300 saved from $3,000 is a 10% savings rate.
    50/30/20 guide
    A simple starting point: about 50% on needs, 30% on wants, 20% on savings. In expensive cities, needs often take more.

    Savings goal

    How long until you hit a goal, and what you'd need to save to hit it by a date.

    Emergency fund

    3 to 6 months of essential costs, kept somewhere safe, so a surprise bill doesn't become debt. Aim higher if your income is irregular.
