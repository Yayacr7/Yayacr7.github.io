FAQ

Is Sharp free?

Yes. All lessons, quizzes and tools are free. The Launch course is $30 (Builder), or $80 with the Founder toolkit and negotiation scripts. Both are one-time payments.

Who is it for?

People with a business idea, or the itch to start one, who want to understand money, marketing, deals and investors without the jargon. No business background needed.

Is it really that simple?

The basics are, and that's most of what you need day to day. Where simplifying would mislead you, such as tax or legal details, the lessons say so and tell you when to ask a professional.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account gets 3 days of Founder access, starting when you create it. No card is needed and nothing renews. After 3 days you keep the free plan, or buy Builder or Founder.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Every lesson and tool works without an account. Signing in keeps your purchases in one place, and it's how you'll open the course.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's education and calculators. For legal, tax or big irreversible decisions, pay a professional who knows your situation.
