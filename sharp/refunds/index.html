Refund policy

30 days. Full refund. No reason needed.

Draft. Sharp isn't open yet. This page will be checked by a legal professional before launch and may change.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

The short version
  • Ask within 30 days of buying and you get all your money back.
  • You don't have to explain why.
  • If something listed in your plan isn't ready within 60 days, you can get a refund after the 30 days too.

How to ask

Email the contact email listed here before launch from the address you bought with. Tell us roughly when you bought. That's it.

What happens next

  • We reply within 2 business days and refund the full amount to the card or account you paid with, through Stripe.
  • Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show the money back.
  • Your access to that plan's paid content ends when the refund is made. The free lessons and tools stay yours.

Other cases

  • Charged twice, or bought by mistake: always refunded, whenever you notice.
  • Something missing from your plan: if anything listed in your plan isn't available within 60 days of your purchase, you can ask for a full refund even after the 30 days.
  • Repeated refunds: if someone buys and refunds again and again, we may say no to a new purchase.

Before a chargeback

Please email us first. A refund from us is faster for you than a bank dispute, and it's always the full amount.
