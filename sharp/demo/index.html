Questions

Is Sharp really free?

Yes. Every lesson and every tool is free, with no sign-up and no card. There are optional paid plans with extra course content and more help from Cherry, but you never need one.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use everything free without one.

Is my money info private?

Yes. The numbers you type into the money tools stay on your device and are never sent to us. When you ask Cherry's AI a question or call her, that message goes to the AI service so she can answer. The privacy policy explains it all.

Will Sharp make me money?

No app can promise that, and Sharp won't. It shows you the path (how to earn, keep and grow money) and does the maths, but results depend on your effort, your skills and your area. Every number is an estimate with its assumption shown.

Is this financial advice?

No. Sharp teaches the basics in simple words. For big personal decisions like investing, taxes or legal questions, talk to a parent or another trusted adult first.check with a qualified professional.

Do I need to be good with money or tech?

No. Everything uses simple words, and Cherry explains anything you're stuck on.

Who made Sharp?

A teen founder who wanted a simple, honest place to learn how to earn, keep and grow money.

How do I contact you?

Email us (see the About page). Questions, ideas and mistakes you spot are all welcome.
