What you charge businesses

Fair prices. Each tier does more.

Start low while you build proof. Every tier includes everything below it. Ad budget is paid by the business, straight to Meta or Google.

First 3 clients: first month at half price, in exchange for an honest review and permission to share results.
Raise prices with proof. After 3 clients with good results, add about $100 to each tier for new clients.
Month to month. No long contracts. Clients stay because the ads work.
Brand results build over 3–6 months. Tell owners that up front, and pair brand work with ads when they need customers sooner.

Your business

Run it like a real agency

You sell results to local businesses. AI does the heavy lifting on copy, images and video.

Your offer

One clear sentence

"I help [type of business] in [city] get more customers with AI-made ads, managed every week, from $199 a month."

Startup cost

$0 to $50

Free: Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, Canva, CapCut, Loom, Google Sheets, Calendly. Later: one paid AI plan (about $20 a month) and a domain (about $12 a year).

Your role

Strategist, not button-pusher

You choose the offer, the audience and what to test. AI writes and designs. The ad platform's AI finds buyers. You read the numbers and improve every week.

Week 1

Learn + build proof

Finish the course. Make 3 demo ads for one niche in the Ad Maker. Run your own $5-a-day test ad to learn Ads Manager.

Weeks 2–4

First clients

Follow the 14-day plan. Offer Starter or Growth with the founding discount. Deliver more than promised.

Month 2+

Grow

Turn results into case studies. Move happy clients up a tier when they want more. Ask each for one referral.

After they say yes (days 0–3)

  • Send a simple agreement and invoice (Stripe, PayPal or Square invoices).
  • Ask for partner access to their Meta Business account. The client owns it.
  • Collect their logo, 10+ real photos, prices, best offer and phone or booking link.
  • Install the Meta Pixel and Google tag, or use an instant form.
  • Agree on one goal number: calls, bookings or sales a month.

Every month

  • Week 1: new creatives from the Ad Maker, launch a 3×3 test.
  • Week 2: pause ads costing 2× the target per lead, move budget to winners.
  • Week 3: new hooks on the winner, ask the owner how lead quality looks.
  • Week 4: send a short report (spend, leads, cost per lead, customers) and the plan for next month.
