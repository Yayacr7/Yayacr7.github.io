What to charge

Prices that win clients and pay you well

Cheaper than a big agency ($2,000–$5,000 a month), more than a cheap freelancer ($300). AI makes you fast, so you can charge a fair middle price and still earn a lot per hour.

Founding-client deal (your first 3)

30% off the monthly fee for 3 months in exchange for a written review and permission to share results. This gets you case studies fast.

Charge more for big-ticket niches

Roofers, dentists, lawyers, HVAC and med spas earn $700–$12,000 per customer. Charge them about 1.5× (Growth becomes $1,497).

Ad spend is separate

The client puts their own card in Facebook or Google. Ask for at least $15–$30 a day. Never mix their ad money with your fee.

Your AI ad business

The business plan

A one-person AI advertising agency. You sell results (more calls, bookings and sales) to local businesses. AI does the heavy lifting on copy, images and video.

Offer

One sentence

"I help [niche] in [city] get 20+ new customers a month with AI-made ads, or you don't pay next month."

Only add a guarantee once you've had results with 2–3 clients.

Startup cost

Under $100

Free: Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, Canva free, CapCut, Loom, Google Sheets, Calendly. Paid later: an AI chat plan (~$20/mo), Canva Pro (~$15/mo), a domain (~$12/yr).

Strategist, not button-pusher

What happens after they say yes

Onboarding checklist (day 0–2)

  1. Send a simple contract and invoice (Stripe, PayPal or Square invoices all work).
  2. Ask to be added as a partner in their Meta Business account, and as a user in their Google Ads. The client owns the accounts.
  3. Collect logo, 10+ real photos, prices, best offer, and their phone or booking link.
  4. Install the Meta Pixel and Google tag on their website (or use a lead form).
  5. Agree on one goal number: calls, bookings or sales per month.

Every month

  1. Week 1: Make 12 new creatives with the Ad Builder. Launch the 3×3 test.
  2. Week 2: Turn off losers (high cost per lead). Move budget to winners.
  3. Week 3: New hooks on the winning image. Check lead quality with the owner.
  4. Week 4: Send a one-page report: spend, leads, cost per lead, sales. Book a 15-min call.

Stay legal and trusted

  • Use a written agreement: what you do, the fee, the notice period (30 days), who pays ad spend.
  • Follow each platform's ad rules. Housing, jobs, credit and politics have special rules.
  • Never write fake reviews or testimonials. In the US the FTC can fine businesses for them.
  • Register a business name and keep records for taxes. Ask a local accountant if unsure.

How to grow past 5 clients

  • Write down every task as a checklist, then hire a part-time assistant for creatives and reports.
  • Raise prices for new clients every 3 wins.
  • White-label: make ads for other agencies at a wholesale price.
  • Ask every happy client for 2 referrals and give them a free month per signed referral.
