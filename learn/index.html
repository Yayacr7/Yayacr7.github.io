Learn business without the jargon

Short lessons on how to negotiate and how money works, then quizzes that tell you exactly where you went wrong. Every term is explained the first time it shows up.

1. Negotiation

BATNA, walk-away price, anchoring, trading instead of conceding. A one-page prep sheet you can use tomorrow.

2. Finance

Personal money, then business money: compound interest, debt, investing, profit vs cash, the three statements, break-even.

3. Check your work

Multiple-choice and real calculations. Each answer shows the reasoning, so a wrong answer still teaches you something.

4. AI prompts

System prompts and master prompts to turn an AI into a tutor, a negotiation sparring partner, or an honest critic.

How to use this site

  1. Read one lesson section. Each takes 5 to 10 minutes.
  2. Take the matching quiz right away. Testing yourself beats re-reading; it is the single most reliable study technique there is.
  3. Read the explanation on every miss. That is where the learning happens.
  4. Practice for real. Use the negotiation sparring prompt to rehearse a real conversation before you have it.
Honest scope note. This covers the fundamentals that most people actually use. It is not everything about finance (nothing short of a degree plus years of work is), and it is education, not personal financial, legal or tax advice.
