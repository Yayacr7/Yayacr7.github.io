The course · planned curriculum

Six modules. Real numbers. No hype.

Built for people who've never read a profit and loss statement. Waitlist members shape the final version: reply to the welcome email and say what you're stuck on.

    Is this idea worth your evenings? Customers needed, price, and the gut-check math.

  2. Price it so it pays you

    Why most first-time founders undercharge, and how to price on value instead of cost.

  3. Unit economics without the jargon

    Bootstrapping vs loans vs investors, what each costs you, and when to say no to money.
