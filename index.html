Story mode

Ten chapters. You start with nothing.

Solo raid

One champion, no help, a ladder of opponents that keeps getting harder.

Every third floor is a sub-boss, and every twentieth is the RAID BOSS — picked from the heaviest hitters on the roster, with by far the biggest health bar in the game. Bosses pay enormously: a sub-boss is worth three floors, the raid boss eleven. Your health carries from floor to floor with a chunk back between fights, and you go into every boss patched up to full. After each floor you choose: go deeper for a bigger pot, or bank what you have. Lose and you still keep half.

Best with this champion: floor 0  ·  Best ever: floor 0

Team battle

Three champions each. When one falls the next steps in.

Pick a squad of three. You fight one at a time, and whoever is still standing keeps the damage they have taken — the next champion in comes on fresh. Beat all three of theirs before they beat all three of yours. Every knockout pays, and clearing the whole squad pays a large bonus on top.

Squads beaten: 0

Survival

Round after round, and nothing heals.

The raid gives you a chunk of health back between floors. This gives you nothing — you carry every scratch to the next round, and the rounds keep getting harder. There is no banking decision either: everything you earn is yours whatever happens, so it is the steadiest way to farm credits in the game. The only thing at stake is the number.

Best run: 0 rounds

Tournament

Eight fighters. Three rounds. One champion.

A straight knockout bracket: quarter-final, semi-final, final. You are healed to full between rounds, but each opponent is tougher than the last and the final is a real fight. Lose at any point and you are out with a small consolation. Win all three and you take the trophy and the whole purse.

    Hall of Fame — your champions

      Move list

      ◀▶ Moving

      • Walk: hold ◀ ▶ (or ← →, or Q E on a keyboard). Punches and kicks only reach so far; out of range they MISS.
      • Dash in: double-tap toward them.
      • Dodge: double-tap away. For a moment nothing can touch you: DODGE!
      • On a phone, pick Buttons, Swipe or Automatic in ⚙️ Settings.

      👊 Punch

      • Jab → Straight → Finisher. Tap three times. The finisher is an uppercut, a body hook, an elbow, a spinning backfist or a headbutt.

      🦶 Kick

      • Each press is a different kick: roundhouse, low kick, push kick, knee strike, axe kick, spinning heel kick, sweep.
      • The sweep knocks them down.
      • Kick someone who is blocking and you throw them instead.

      🚀 Jump

      • Jump, then Punch: Flying Punch.
      • Jump, then Kick: Flying Kick.
      • Jump, then Power: Flying Knee (25% meter). Knocks them down.

      ⚡ Power

      • Half meter: Punch of Death. Knocks them down.
      • Full meter: your champion's Super Move.

      🛡️ Defence

      • Your champion blocks on their own some of the time. Hold S to block yourself.
      • Block right as the hit arrives for a PARRY: no damage, and they are left wide open.
      • Block too much and your guard breaks.

      ☠️ Finisher

      • Full meter and your opponent under 25% health? POWER turns into a FINISHER: a five-hit cinematic that ends the fight.

      🥋 Fighting styles

      • 🥊 Boxer: punches +20%
      • 🦵 Kickboxer: kicks +20%
      • 🤼 Wrestler: kicks often become throws, and throws +40%
      • 👊 Brawler: knocks them back further and dizzies faster
      • 🥋 Martial Artist: flying attacks +25%
      • 💨 Speedster: recovers 20% faster
      • 🦍 Powerhouse: Punch of Death costs 40%

      ⭐ Big moments

      • COUNTER! Hit them while they wind up: +35% damage.
      • AIR COMBO! Hit them while they are in the air.
      • DIZZY! Enough hits in a row and they see stars.
      • RAGE MODE! Under 30% health, you hit 25% harder.
      • EDGE BOUNCE! Knock them into the edge of the platform.
      • PERFECT! Win without taking a hit: +50% credits.
