One champion, no help, a ladder of opponents that keeps getting harder.

Every third floor is a sub-boss, and every twentieth is the RAID BOSS — picked from the heaviest hitters on the roster, with by far the biggest health bar in the game. Bosses pay enormously: a sub-boss is worth three floors, the raid boss eleven. Your health carries from floor to floor with a chunk back between fights, and you go into every boss patched up to full. After each floor you choose: go deeper for a bigger pot, or bank what you have. Lose and you still keep half.

Land hits to fill your meter; guarding fills it slowly too. At 25% you can throw a Super Punch that hits twice as hard. At 100% you unleash that champion's Super Move — every one is different, and each is that hero's signature move. Superman can also take off with a Flying Jump — he climbs higher than anyone and dives down fist-first. And Batman fights with his head: his Intellect bar fills as he watches an opponent attack, quietly raising his damage, and a full read lands a Predicted Counter for more than double. Every bout pays credits and experience. Experience levels your champion up on its own, and credits buy the two upgrades: Train for an instant level, or Forge armour to push your champion up a finish — Standard, Polished, Chrome, Energised, then Legendary. Each forge makes them dramatically stronger and dramatically shinier.
